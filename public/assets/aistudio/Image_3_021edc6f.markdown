## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for high-precision atmospheric monitoring, biosensor telemetry, and macro-climate intelligence platforms. The visual posture merges laboratory instrument clarity with contemporary telemetry aesthetics. It balances critical infrastructure dependability with luminous, dynamic environmental feedback.

### Emotional Disposition
- **Clinical Precision:** Zero visual ambiguity; metric values, unit scalar notations, and geographic timestamps read instantly without cognitive load.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations punctured by selective, highly calibrated radiant signals that mirror optical spectrum instrumentation.
- **Authoritative & Scientific:** Communicates peer-reviewed sensor accuracy, real-time computational vigor, and planetary-scale operational awareness.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Deep-Field Technical Slate** with **Targeted Spectral Glassmorphism**. Structural framing relies on hairline borders, muted telemetry tracks, and translucent panels that reveal underlying situational contours without compromising contrast or data legibility.

## Colors

The palette is engineered strictly for dark environments to prioritize retinal comfort during prolonged situational monitoring, sensor diagnostic runs, and nocturnal command shifts.

### Atmospheric Foundation & Tonal Layers
- **Surface Canvas (Base):** `#0b0f19` (Obsidian Deep Slate) – Foundation tier absorbing light to maximize foreground lumen contrast.
- **Surface Layer 1 (Cards & Sidebars):** `#111827` (Charcoal Navy) – Structured container tier with a nominal opacity baseline of 80% when glass effect is active.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Raised Panels / Flyouts):** `#1e293b` (Mid-Slate) – Distinct interactive elevation zone.
- **Border Structural / Hairline:** `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)` – Delimiting gridlines, retaining crisp enclosure without optical weight.
- **Border Radiant / Focus:** `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45)` – State changes, highlighted sensor ranges, and selected inputs.

### Core Primary Accents
- **Primary Teal-Cyan (`#06b6d4`):** Primary interaction elements, live stream telemetry ticks, active states, and focal tracking charts.
- **Secondary Mint-Teal (`#14b8a6`):** Secondary delta metrics, positive flux vectors, and system synchronization indicators.
- **Tertiary Atmospheric Violet (`#8b5cf6`):** Stratospheric telemetry, particulate tracking (PM1.0/VOC indices), and tertiary data sets.

### Environmental Categorical Spectrum (AQI & Alert States)
- **Optimal / Nominal (Good AQI 0–50):** `#10b981` (Emerald)
- **Caution / Elevated (Moderate AQI 51–100):** `#f59e0b` (Amber)
- **Unhealthy / High Vector (Unhealthy AQI 101–200):** `#f43f5e` (Rose)
- **Hazardous / Severe (Hazardous AQI 201+):** `#a855f7` (Violet)

### Text & Telemetry Contrast
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#f8fafc` – Metric integers, primary chart axes, critical alerts.
- **Text Medium-Contrast:** `#94a3b8` – Section captions, standard readouts, secondary descriptors.
- **Text Low-Contrast / Meta:** `#64748b` – Unit definitions (e.g., `µg/m³`, `ppm`), historical timestamps, inactive states.

## Typography

Typography is bifurcated into two specialized functional domains: structural human language and machine-rendered metric precision.

### Functional Roles
- **Headlines & Executive Metric Groups (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Smooth geometric tracking with open apertures. Delivers modern visual clarity to station summaries, alert modals, and high-level dashboard section headers.
- **Analytical Prose & Controls (`Inter`):** Neutral, utilitarian, and high legibility across diverse data densities. Governs standard telemetry configuration, diagnostic copy, and settings.
- **Numerical Arrays, Code & Timestamps (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width baseline eliminates layout shifting during continuous stream updates. Applied strictly to raw sensor readouts, coordinate matrices (lat/long), micro-averages, and delta percentages.

### Typography Application Rules
- Numerical digits must utilize tabular lining figures (`tnum`) across all fonts to maintain column alignment within data tables.
- Units of measure (`ppm`, `ppb`, `AQI`, `mg/m³`) must follow values set at `label-data-sm` with a secondary color tone (`#64748b`) to prevent analytical confusion with the main magnitude.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured fluid-grid framework designed for dynamic high-density multi-display environments (from mobile alerts to ultra-wide operations centers).

### Layout Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column adaptive grid with `margin-desktop` (2rem) and `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem). Modules dynamically nest into 3-column, 4-column, or 6-column spatial panels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column adaptive grid with `margin-tablet` (1.5rem) and standard `gutter` (1rem). Dense sensor sidebars collapse into toggleable glass drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stream stack with `margin` (1rem) and `gutter` (1rem). High-priority readouts prioritize single-value full-width cards with micro-sparklines.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Inner card padding follows a consistent `space-md` (1rem) to `space-lg` (1.5rem) rhythm to preserve readability alongside high analytical density.
- Data pairs (label + value) require compact pairing via `space-xxs` (2px) to `space-xs` (4px) to retain strong perceptual grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through luminous surface stratification rather than traditional high-opacity dropped shadows. Surfaces recede or advance through transparency, border luminance, and subtle backdrop diffusion.

### Elevation Hierarchy

1. **Canvas Layer (Base, 0dp):**
   - Solid `#0b0f19` background. Serves as the substrate for charting grids and vector coordinate overlays.

2. **Panel / Sensor Tile Tier (Layer 1, 1dp):**
   - Background: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(12px)`.
   - Border: Hairline `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.1)`.
   - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

3. **Active Panel / Hovered Module (Layer 2, 2dp):**
   - Background: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)`.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 15px 0 rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`.

4. **Floating Overlays & Critical Alerts (Layer 3, 3dp):**
   - Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95)`.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`.
   - Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** structural rounding pattern to reinforce technical discipline, maximize data layout efficiency, and echo precision laboratory hardware.

### Border Radius Implementation
- **Micro UI (Badges, Checkboxes, Toggle switches):** `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px).
- **Core Structural Units (Data Cards, Analytics Panels, Inputs):** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Modal Dialogs & Context Windows:** `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px).
- **Status Indicators & Sensor Pulse Dots:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to maintain standard geometric parity with radial sensor telemetry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Telemetry Action:** Gradient border fill with background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`, text `#06b6d4`, border `1px solid #06b6d4`. On hover, background shifts to `#06b6d4` with text `#0b0f19` alongside a localized `0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary Action:** Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`, text `#f8fafc`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`. Hover elevates border to `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.3)`.
- **Destructive / Alert Action:** Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`, text `#f43f5e`, border `1px solid #f43f5e`.

### Telemetry Cards & Panels
- Constructed from Layer 1 elevation with `rounded-lg` (8px). 
- Header features sensor identifier and real-time status pip (6px circle with a subtle concentric expanding pulse animation).
- Metrics display prominently in `label-data-lg` alongside secondary unit definitions in `label-data-sm`.

### Status Badges & Category Chips
- Pill-inspired compact modules (`rounded` 4px) containing categorical AQI definitions.
- Background uses corresponding status color at 12% opacity (e.g., `#10b981` at 0.12).
- Font rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-data-sm`, 11px) using full saturation text color, paired with a leading 4px solid status dot.

### Data Inputs & Filter Bars
- Inputs feature background `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#f8fafc`, font `Inter` (`body-md`).
- Focus state triggers border transition to `#06b6d4` and a radial cyan outline shadow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #06b6d4`).
- Monospace scalar selectors feature quick-stepper decrement/increment keys with tactile border-left dividers.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom checkboxes are 16x16px squares with `rounded` (4px), dark slate base, and teal checked fill with sharp white micro-checkmarks.
- Segmented time-window selectors (e.g., `1H`, `24H`, `7D`, `30D`) are enclosed in a contiguous `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)` chassis, with active segments surfaced in `#1e293b` with a hairline cyan bottom track.

### Sensor Data Visualizations & Sparklines
- Chart lines leverage dynamic SVG paths with a stroke width of 1.5px to 2px.
- Primary telemetry series renders in `#06b6d4` with a vertical linear gradient fill descending to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.0)`.
- Crosshairs render as crisp 1px dashed rules in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)` accompanied by floating glass tooltips styled via Layer 3 depth specifications.
